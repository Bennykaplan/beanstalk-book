Privacy

Beanstalk Storybook is a student project for a university course. It isn't a commercial service.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store your Google account ID, name and email address. We store the details you type for the About Me page, the pictures generated for your book, and a log of each image made.

Your photo

The photo you upload is sent to OpenAI once, to draw the character in your book. It's kept in memory only while that happens and isn't saved in our database. OpenAI handles it under its own policies. The generated pictures are also made by OpenAI from that character drawing.

What we do with it

We use this information only to make your book and show it to you when you come back. We don't sell it or use it for advertising. Other users can't see your books.

Children

This site is meant to be used by an adult. Please upload a photo of a child only if you are their parent or guardian, or have their permission.

Deleting your data

Use "Delete this book" on your book's page to remove it and its pictures right away. To remove your account as well, open an issue on the project's GitHub page without including any personal details, and we'll arrange it.
